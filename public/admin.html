Co-op setup

one continuous main.c: Player 1's code, then Player 2's
The program is one file: Player 1 writes the top (the #includes and the start of main()), and Player 2 carries on right after it, using Player 1's variables. Player 2's line numbers continue from Player 1's.
Each player has their own hidden tests (set them in the Test cases tab), so one player can't do all the work.
The #include <…> lines at the top of Player 1's starter are shown greyed out and locked.
